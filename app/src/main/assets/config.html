Quick actions

Hold the remote's Netflix button to open these over any app. Each calls a Home Assistant service, e.g. scene.turn_on with {"entity_id": "scene.movie"}. Leave the service blank for a placeholder. Icons use Home Assistant's names, e.g. mdi:movie-open.

Cameras

Double-press the Netflix button to see these over any app. Tick the cameras to show and put them in order; with none ticked, every camera in Home Assistant is shown.

Background

One of the images bundled with the launcher, chosen from Home Assistant's weather, sun and season and the date. Checked every 5 minutes while the home screen is showing.

Holidays

One per line, name: from..to or a single day; the first match wins and replaces the weather image. Days are MM-DD, thanksgiving (fourth Thursday of November) or easter, optionally +N/-N days. A range like 12-31..01-01 wraps over New Year. Names match the image folders.
